========================================================================
PART 3. Writing
========================================================================

A. Take the balance on a finished order.
   The payment is added, the totals change and the status moves on,
   all in one update of one document. No transaction is needed.

   before: order 94599  status ready  paid 8,600.00  balance 8,600.00
   after:  order 94599  status collected  paid 17,200.00  balance 0.00  payments 2

B. Sell ready-to-wear stock, but only if there is enough.
   The stock test is part of the update's filter, so checking and
   changing cannot be separated by another session.

   KAF-CLS-L-BLK has 9 in stock.
   Sell 9: done
   Sell 1 more: refused, nothing changed

C. Record details that only some garments have.
   Two styles gain an 'attributes' object with different fields,
   and a list of tags. The other styles are not touched.

   Three-piece agbada   {"pieces":3,"embroidery":"hand, chest and sleeves","cap":true}
   A-line dress         {"neckline":"V","length":"knee","lined":true}
   Styles with attributes: 2 of 14
